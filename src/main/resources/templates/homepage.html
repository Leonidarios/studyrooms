📚 StudyRooms

Book a study room at the HUA library

The StudyRooms web application modernizes academic communication at Harokopio University. Students can easily book studying space in the library, and receive real-time notifications — all from one intuitive interface. Library staff get a streamlined dashboard to manage studying rooms efficiently.

With secure authentication, automated reminders, and seamless integration with university services, StudyRooms brings clarity, speed, and transparency to everyday otherwise-pesky arrangements. An easier and more convenient way to find and book your much needed studying space!
